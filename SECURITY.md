A bug in the Peachy bot SDK or protocol itself goes to Peachy, under
https://pchy.app/security.

If you run this bot and its store or store key may have leaked, reset the
bot's keys in Peachy: Settings › Organizations and bots › your bot › Link a
new server (on Mac, You › Organizations and bots).
